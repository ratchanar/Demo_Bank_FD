How the calculation works

This demo uses simple interest, not compound interest.

1. Simple interest
Principal × Annual Rate × Term ÷ 100
2. Maturity amount
Principal + Simple Interest

Example: ₹100,000 at 7% for 2 years gives interest of ₹14,000 and a maturity amount of ₹114,000. Actual bank products may use different day-count conventions, tax rules or compounding methods.
